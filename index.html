Cooked the way we were raised

TasteLocal began at a family table, where Sunday meant a pot of sinigang and too many cousins. We opened to bring that table to the city.

Our pork comes from small farms, our fish from the Davao Gulf, and our vinegar from a family that has fermented it for three generations. We do not reinvent Filipino food. We cook it carefully and serve it warmly.

Authentic flavor
Recipes passed down, not rewritten.
Local ingredients
Sourced within Mindanao wherever possible.
Warm hospitality
Everyone is treated like family.
